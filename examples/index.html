What the kit enforces

Four laws cover geometry, contract, motion and restraint. Around them: typography floors, a status rule, square-capped icons. Each is short enough to remember; the audits make them hard to break.

Hard limits

  • No rounded rectangles — the only curve allowed is a true circle (the radio control).
  • 12px text floor on CJK, 10px on Latin.
  • Hit area: at least 24px for a mouse, 44px on touch screens.

Law 1

Align within the owning layout

Page frames, regions and component interiors have distinct alignment scopes. Share tracks with subgrid when needed; local layouts may use their own content-driven structure.

Law 2

Similar elements reuse one contract

A button is a button everywhere. Variants are named attributes, states come from semantic markup — never from a parallel class that can drift.

Law 3

Motion returns to its origin

Panels open from where the user was looking and give the space back when they close. Nothing slides off the page forever.

Law 4

Minimal, weighted, icon-first

Show only what the task needs and give each item one weight. Where an established icon names an action, it stands alone; the words move into its tooltip.
