KFL Grid Demo

A reference page for the grid-first workflow: declare the grid in a manifest, generate the substrate, place every region on a track, then audit the rendered geometry.

Why a manifest comes first

The manifest is the contract. Columns, gutter, margin, and container are declared once per breakpoint, and the generator turns that declaration into tokens, layout classes, and the debug overlay. Elements placed later can only inherit from it.

An asymmetric 5/4 split is used here: the article is held near a 66-character measure, the rail takes its functional width, and three tracks stay empty because the container is wider than the content needs. Equal splits are reserved for genuinely parallel content.

Reading the audit

The auditor measures rendered edges against the manifest's track lines and fails within one pixel of drift. A passing report is the acceptance evidence for Law 1.
